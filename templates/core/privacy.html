Privacy Policy & Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2026. University capstone project, 2026–2027.

Medical Disclaimer

DermaCheckerBD is an experimental research prototype developed as part of a university capstone project. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide a professional medical diagnosis.

All predictions, confidence scores, image quality assessments, stability metrics, and multi-image comparisons generated by this tool are experimental and may be inaccurate. They are intended for educational and research purposes only and must not be used as the sole basis for medical decisions. A prediction or confidence score does not confirm or rule out skin cancer. If you notice a new, changing, bleeding, painful, or otherwise concerning skin lesion, consult a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional.

What Data We Collect

  • Account information: Username, optional name and email address, and securely hashed password.
  • Uploaded images: Skin lesion or other images you upload for image quality assessment or experimental analysis.
  • Prediction records: Predicted class, confidence score, image quality report, and related analysis results, where applicable.
  • Chatbot messages: Questions and messages you submit to the AI chatbot.
  • Technical logs: Request paths, response status codes, timestamps, and other technical metadata required for application operation, security, and troubleshooting.

Please avoid including unnecessary personal information in uploaded images or chatbot messages. Do not submit another person's personal information without appropriate permission.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide account access and application functionality.
  • To process uploaded images and display experimental results.
  • To save and display your prediction history, where supported.
  • To provide chatbot responses to your submitted questions.
  • To monitor technical errors, prevent abuse, and improve application reliability.
  • To support the educational and research objectives of the capstone project.

Your information should not be interpreted as being used for clinical diagnosis or treatment. Any research use beyond the operation of this application should follow applicable university requirements and relevant privacy and research rules.

Data Retention

Uploaded images and prediction records may be retained according to the application's configured storage and retention policies. Uploaded images may be kept for the lifetime of your account by default if that behavior is enabled. The application operator may configure an image retention period through the IMAGE_RETENTION_DAYS setting, where implemented.

You can delete saved lesion-tracking records through the Follow-up Tracker page if that feature is available. Deleting an individual record may not automatically delete backups, technical logs, or copies retained by third-party providers. You may contact the project maintainer to request account deletion or ask questions about stored information.

Third-Party Services

DermaCheckerBD may rely on third-party services to operate. The data processed by each service depends on the application's actual configuration and the feature you use.

  • Google Gemini API: Chatbot messages and relevant contextual information may be sent to Google's API to generate responses. When a signed-in user asks about their own dashboard or history, a limited summary of their recent predictions or tracked lesions may be included. Authorized staff questions may include anonymized, site-wide counts only. Usernames, contact details, images, and individual patient records are not included in staff context.
  • Neon PostgreSQL: May host the application's production database, including account and application records.
  • Render: May host the web application and process requests made to the service.
  • Content delivery networks: Bootstrap CSS, Lucide icons, or other frontend resources may be loaded from providers such as jsDelivr and unpkg. These providers may receive standard connection metadata when your browser requests their resources.

Third-party providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Please review those policies when appropriate. The project maintainer does not control every aspect of third-party data processing.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are intended to help protect application data. Depending on the deployed configuration, these measures may include:

  • HTTPS and secure-cookie settings in production.
  • CSRF protection for applicable forms and state-changing requests.
  • File-type and file-size validation for image uploads.
  • Rate limiting on supported endpoints to help prevent abuse.
  • API keys and secrets stored in environment variables rather than exposed in frontend code.
  • User-specific access restrictions intended to prevent users from accessing other users' private records.

No website, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Security measures depend on the current implementation and deployment configuration.

Your Rights and Choices

Subject to the application's capabilities and applicable requirements, you may be able to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • View your own prediction history and saved records.
  • Delete supported saved lesion-tracking records.
  • Request correction or deletion of account-related information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is processed.

To request account deletion or raise a privacy concern, contact the project maintainer using the email address provided below. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate technical, security, or legal purposes.

Cookies and Sessions

The application may use cookies or session mechanisms to maintain login status, support security protections, and preserve essential application functionality. Browser settings can be used to control cookies, although disabling essential cookies may prevent some features from working correctly.

Children's Privacy

This research prototype is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information unnecessarily, please contact the project maintainer so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, data processing practices, or project requirements change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page. Users should review this page periodically for updates.
