About the project

About DermaCheckerBD

An experimental web app for reviewing skin-lesion photos and organizing follow-up observations. It is not a clinical tool.

What the project is for

DermaCheckerBD brings photo guidance, experimental image analysis, result comparisons, and follow-up tools together in one place. Its goal is to make the results and their limitations easier to understand, not to provide a diagnosis.

What the app offers

  • Photo guidance with suggestions when an image is blurry, poorly lit, or difficult to frame.
  • An experimental predicted category and confidence score, shown with clear limitations.
  • Optional comparisons across small photo changes or multiple images; differences are not proof of disease or correctness.
  • A private follow-up timeline where signed-in users can organize photos and dates.
  • The Doctor Panel lets authorized doctors and administrators review cases, update status, and generate serial reports.
  • An assistant that explains terms and app limitations, but does not diagnose or prescribe treatment.

Responsible use

This prototype has not been clinically validated. Predictions and confidence scores are unverified and may be wrong. Consistency across checks does not mean a result is medically correct.

Privacy and access

Follow-up records are private to the signed-in account. Doctor Panel access is limited to authorized doctors and administrators. Uploaded images are not sent to the on-page assistant.

Important: DermaCheckerBD does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional care. If you are concerned about a skin lesion, consult a qualified dermatologist.
