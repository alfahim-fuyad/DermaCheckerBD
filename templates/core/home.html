Responsible AI

What this prototype is — and isn't

DermaCheckerBD is an experimental research prototype. It is not a medical device, not clinically validated, and not a substitute for professional dermatology evaluation.

The model can be wrong even when it produces a high confidence score. Stability and multi-image agreement describe the model's behaviour, not whether the prediction is medically correct. If a lesion is changing, bleeding, growing, or otherwise concerning, please consult a qualified dermatologist promptly.
