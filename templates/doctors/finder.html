How this works

  • Your browser asks for location permission. Coordinates are sent to our server, which calls Geoapify — never directly from the browser.
  • Results come from OpenStreetMap via the Geoapify Places API: hospitals, clinics, and dermatologists tagged by the OSM community.
  • Up to {{ max_results }} facilities are shown, sorted by distance. No fabricated names, ratings, or hours.
  • If location is denied, type an area name — we geocode it via Geoapify and search from there.
